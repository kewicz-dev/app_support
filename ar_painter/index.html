Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Why doesn't the AR camera wall-painting feature work on my device?

Real-time surface painting requires an Apple device equipped with a LiDAR Scanner (iPhone 12 Pro / 13 Pro / 14 Pro / 15 Pro / 16 Pro models or iPad Pro 2020 and newer). On devices without LiDAR, you can still use all color catalogs, converters, and full-screen color previews.

2. How do I restore my purchased color palettes?

Open the app, go to the Settings or palette store screen, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

3. Which color systems are included?

The catalog includes RAL Classic, RAL Design, NCS, Benjamin Moore, Sherwin Williams, BEHR PRO, PPG Voice of Color, Caparol 3D System Plus, Valspar, Dunn Edwards, Hallman Lindsay, DIC, and Matthews Paint.

4. System Requirements

Requires iOS 16.0 or later, iPadOS 16.0 or later, macOS 13.0 or later (Apple M1 chip or newer), or visionOS 1.0 or later.
