Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Does ZINO IPTV provide built-in TV channels or playlists?

No. ZINO IPTV is a media player only and does not include any pre-loaded channels or media content. You need to add your own M3U playlist URL or local file provided by your service provider.

2. Why won't a specific stream load?

Check your internet connection and verify that your M3U link or EPG source URL is still active and has not expired with your provider.

3. How do I restore my purchases?

Open the app Settings screen and tap Restore Purchases using the same Apple ID used for the initial purchase.
