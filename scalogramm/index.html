Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I get the most accurate weight estimation?

Place your device on a flat, stable, and slightly flexible surface (such as a folded towel or cushion) and follow the on-screen calibration instructions before placing an object.

2. How do I restore my purchases?

Open the app Settings screen and tap Restore Purchases using the same Apple ID you used originally.
