Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I change the units of measurement (metric or imperial)?

Open the Settings screen inside the app to choose your preferred units for dimensions, weight, and calculation results.

2. How should I enter fractional dimensions (e.g., 1/4 inch)?

Enter values in decimal format using a dot or comma depending on your regional keyboard settings (for example, type 0.25 or 0,25 instead of leaving the leading zero out).

3. Can I add custom metals and save my calculations?

Yes. You can create custom materials with your own density values and save any calculation along with its units to the Favorites section. All features work completely offline.

4. How do I restore my in-app purchases?

Go to the Settings screen inside the app and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

5. System Requirements

Requires iOS 15.0 or later, iPadOS 15.0 or later, macOS 12.0 or later (Apple M1 chip or newer), watchOS 10.0 or later, or visionOS 1.0 or later.
